AGENT COMMISSION

50% of the dispatch fee on the first weekly invoice issued to each eligible carrier the Agent onboards, counted only on the amount the Company actually receives for that invoice. Commission is paid once per carrier, on that first weekly invoice only. No commission is paid on later invoices. An eligible carrier is one that (a) was submitted by the Agent through the Portal after a real conversation, (b) signs the Carrier-Dispatcher Agreement, (c) becomes active, and (d) pays the dispatch fee on its first weekly invoice.

PAYMENT

The Agent's commission is paid after the Company receives the carrier's payment for that first weekly invoice. If a carrier pays late, the commission is paid once the carrier's payment is actually received. There is no fixed salary, no minimum payment and no guaranteed income. Commission depends only on active carriers that pay their first weekly invoice.

TRAINING AND CAREER PATH

Training is provided through the Academy inside the Portal (course chapters, quizzes, auto-generated certificate on completion, Truck Reference and Comparison, and a shared FAQ). Agents who perform strongly may later be trained as Dispatchers under separate terms. This is an opportunity, not a promise.

LEAD ASSIGNMENT AND DAILY WORK

The Admin assigns leads to the Agent directly from the Dashboard. The Agent works assigned leads to find a potential carrier, and submits a lead through the Portal once it is serious or the carrier has shared truck/trailer, accessories, preference or document details. The Admin tracks the Agent's daily and monthly performance in the Portal.

LEADS, DATA AND CONFIDENTIALITY

All leads, carrier records, scripts, course content and Portal data belong to the Company, regardless of who found or contacted them. The Agent will access this information only through the Portal and only for Company work, and will not copy, export, screenshot, download, forward it, or share login details with anyone.

QUITTING AND TERMINATION

An Agent who wants to quit must send written notice to the Administration of the Company at least 2 weeks before quitting. During the notice period the Agent continues assigned work, logs every lead, and hands over open follow-ups; Portal access ends on the last day. Commission already earned is still paid, including on a carrier submitted by the Agent whose first weekly invoice is still unpaid when the Agent leaves.
